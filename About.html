About Us

Get to know us


The South African Education Project (SAEP) is a South african non-profit organisation based in Phillipi in Cape Town. SAEP has been providing education, life-skills and psycological support to children, youth and education providerssince 1994. It's programs are designed to expand educational opportunities and assist youth in aqcquuiring the skills needed for future education and employment. SAEP currently has more than 31 000 beneficiaries and provides five learing programs.

Misson and Vision statement

Mission: to promote access to quality education for children, young adolescents and adults from marginalised communities through education, life skills and the psycho-social support.
Vision:a generation of motivated youth, equipped with education and life-skills to maximise their potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

Learning programmes

  1. Early Childhood Development (ECD):this programme supports under resourced early childhood development centres and helps improve learning environments for young children.
  2. Hope Scholars Programme:supports high school learners through life-skills development, environmental education career guidance and educational excursions.
  3. Bridging Year Programme:helps matriculants transition from school to higher education by providing academic preparation, life-skills and support for tertiary studies.
