Get to know us

Who we are

The South African Education Project (SAEP) is a South african non-profit organisation based in Phillipi in Cape Town. SAEP has been providing education, life-skills and psycological support to children, youth and education providers since 1994.It's programs are designed to expand educational opportunities and assist youth in aqcquuiring the skills needed for future education and employment. SAEP currently has more than 31 000 beneficiaries and provides five learing programs.

Our Mission

To promote access to quality education for children, young adolescents and adults from marginalised communities through education, life skills and the psycho-social support.

Our Vision

A generation of motivated youth, equipped with education and life-skills to maximise their potential and contribute meaningfully to society.
