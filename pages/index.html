Who we are

The South African Education Project (SAEP) is a non-profit organisation based in Phillipi, Cape Town. We have been providing education, life-skills, and psychological support to children and youth education providers since 1994.

Our programmes are designed to expand educational opportunities and assisit youth in acquiring the skills needed for future education and employment.

Learn more about us

Our programmes

  1. Early Childhood Development (ECD) Supports under-resourced early childhood development centres and improves learning environments for young children.
  2. Hope Scholars Programme Supports high school learners through life-skills development, environmental education, career guidance, and educational excursions.
  3. Bridging Year Programme Helps matriculants transition from school to higher education by providing academic preparation, life-skills and support for tertiary studies.
