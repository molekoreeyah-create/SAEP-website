Services by us

  • Education Support
    • We offer tutoring and learning activities to help learners improve their school performance.
  • Literacy
    • Reading groups and activities that strengthen reading comprehension and encourage a love of reading.
  • Life Skills
    • Activities that build confidence, resillience, responsibility and personal development.
  • Psychological Support
    • Social-worker access, parent meetings, home visits and referrals for learners facing barriers to education.
  • Career Guidance
    • Helping learners understand career options and make informed decisions about their future.
  • Environmental Education
    • Hikes, camps and environmental activities that expose learners to nature and environmental careers.
  • ECD Support
    • Training and support for educators and under-resourced Early Childhood Development centres.
  • Tertiary Support
    • Helping learners transition into and succeed in higher education.
